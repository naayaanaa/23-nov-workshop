festival About the Festival

Tradition & Expression

Welcome to the flagship event of the academic spring calendar! The Spring Cultural Fest 2024 unites over 2,500 students, faculty, and visiting regional college troupes for an unforgettable evening of live artistic expression, pulse-pounding collegiate band face-offs, and multi-cultural culinary pavilions.

From the fiercely competitive Inter-Department Dance Showcase to our guest headlining indie-rock set by The Echo Meridian, this annual showcase champions community, diversity, and student craftsmanship. Wander through authentic artisan pop-ups, savor student club food kitchens, and immerse yourself in the luminous spirit of our university heritage.

Stage 01 • Main Bowl
Opening Ceremony & Welcome Note 5:30 PM — 6:15 PM

Commencement address by Dean of Student Affairs, ceremonial lamp lighting, and a showcase anthem by the Campus Choir.

Inter-Department Dance Battle 6:15 PM — 7:30 PM

8 department crews compete in traditional folk fusion, contemporary hip-hop, and freestyle street dance rounds judged by alumni artists.

Headlining Act
Headliner Indie Rock Band Live Performance 7:45 PM — 9:15 PM

National touring act "The Echo Meridian" takes the stage with guest acoustic collaborations featuring student guitar society leads.

DJ Night & Award Distribution 9:30 PM — 10:30 PM

Annual trophy handovers for Department of the Year, followed by an open-air EDM and synthwave set by DJ Kryo.
